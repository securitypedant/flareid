## 9. [MEDIUM] Per-slug service-bind attempt counter counts successes, never clears, and caps total binds at 20/15min - 21 unauthenticated binds to cn=<slug>,ou=services lock the app out, and a busy app locks itself out

- **Fingerprint:** `flareid/ldap/connection.ts:svc-slug-attempt-counter-enables-preauth-lockout`
- **Sink:** src/ldap/bind.ts:99
- **Confidence:** high
- **Root cause:** src/ldap/bind.ts:95-97 - `recordRateAttempt(env, 'ldap:svc:' + resolved.slug, 20, 15)` runs before the secret comparison and counts every bind to the slug, with no clear-on-success path anywhere in the file; rate-limit.ts:52-70 shows recordAttempt is a plain fixed-window increment.
- **Impact:** Denies directory binds for one application for window-length periods - downstream app logins (Grafana/GitLab/SSSD) fail - but is self-healing and scoped to a single app at a time.
- **Likelihood:** Unauthenticated, ~21 requests, and a slug the attacker can guess or read from documentation; repetition sustains the outage across windows.

Before any credential check, ldapBind runs recordRateAttempt on `ldap:svc:<slug>` (20 per 15-min fixed window) and `ldap:svcip:<ip>` (60 per 15-min). recordAttempt is a fixed-window counter incremented on EVERY attempt and is never cleared on success (unlike recordLoginAttempt/clearLoginAttempts for user binds).
